    Controls

    Use − / + in the furniture list to set how many of each piece are in the room.
    Drag a piece to move it; edges snap to walls and other pieces.
    Drag the round handle (front of the piece) to rotate in 15° steps.
    Alt while dragging turns snapping off.
    ←↑→↓ nudge · Shift = 1'
    R rotate 90° · Shift+R back 90°
    [ ] rotate 15° · Del remove · Esc deselect
    Thick edge = back of the piece; dot = its back-left corner (the saved x/y).
    3D view (top right) opens a walk-through window that follows the plan live.
